# UniFi Mount: a family of functional connections

The modular camera arm combines recurring fork-and-tongue interfaces, printed adjustment joints and wall or tripod mounting variants. Many historical Workshop design rules originated in this project.

## Rules from the shared form

Adjacent contours meet flush. Dimensions are derived from one another rather than chosen to be approximately equal. Silhouettes use continuous profiles where possible; edge finishing follows the union of the base solids.

The project-specific preference for printed hand-operated threads is a useful option, not a general prohibition on metal parts. The tripod adapter, for example, includes an intended purchased ball joint.

## Evidence and variants

Assembly assertions check selected contacts and clearances. A separate Manifold experiment compares an alternative modelling route with the OCC parts. Comparing dimensions alone does not establish complete equivalence of all surfaces.

## Transferable rule

A connection pattern includes its function, interface and limitations. Its historical edge family is an optional profile. New materials, loads and print poses require their own evidence.
