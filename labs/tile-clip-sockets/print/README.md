## Print settings (Bambu Lab A1, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA)

- Layer height 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, 15% infill.
- Clips: flat orientation, **no brim, no raft**, no supports.
- Tiles/walls/cube: brim optional (helps the small footprint of the standing wall), no supports --
  every downward-facing pocket feature below is a bridge or a shallow overhang, not an enclosed void.
- `clip-37S-onedge.stl`: no supports; expect a rougher jaw-slot surface than the flat print, that is
  the point of the comparison.

## Orientation and printability

Every pocket has a downward-facing ceiling somewhere; the table below lists the actual bridge/overhang
spans (computed from the pocket dimensions, not a mesh/slicer analysis -- see `results/merged.json`
key `printability_report` for the same table with the raw numbers).

| Coupon | Pose | Feature | Span (mm) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| coupon-merged | tile flat (top up) | mouth bridge | 14.0 | 14 x 2.0 mm bridge over the mouth cut, at the floor (z 1.4) |
| coupon-merged-post | tile flat (top up) | chamber roof | 5.4 | top plate (1.2 mm) over the open chamber; the post at x=8.0 splits the 12.8 mm span into two ~5.4 mm half-bridges instead of one 12.8 mm span |
| coupon-merged-nopost | tile flat (top up) | chamber roof | 12.8 | same 1.2 mm top plate, no post: one unsupported 12.8 mm bridge |
| coupon-openlock-ref | tile flat (top up) | chamber roof | 12.8 | control: same top-plate mechanism as coupon-merged-nopost, 12.8 mm bridge |
| wall-merged / wall-openlock-ref | standing (on its base) | base pocket roof | 14.0 | the base through-pocket's mouth/taper/neck ceiling is a 14 mm bridge near the top of the standing wall |
| wall-merged / wall-openlock-ref | standing (on its base) | side-lock chamber | 4.2 | the vertical side-lock pocket is a through-slot in thickness; each layer bridges only 4.2 mm |
| wall-merged / wall-openlock-ref | lying (on one 25.4 x 25.4 face) | side-lock chamber | 10.52 | rotated 90 deg, the side-lock chamber's 10.52 mm height becomes the bridge span; wall-merged's post sits mid-span and acts as a support, wall-openlock-ref has none |
| wall-merged / wall-openlock-ref | lying (on one 25.4 x 25.4 face) | base through-pocket | 0.0 | prints as a plan profile (the pocket's depth axis is now vertical, its floor/ceiling faces are vertical too): no bridge |
| cube-merged-50.8 | one face down (bottom pocket up against the bed) | bottom-face mouth + chamber roof | 12.8 | the bottom-face pocket opens downward into the bed contact face: its mouth and chamber ceilings print as bridges/overhangs over the void the nozzle cannot reach cleanly on layer 1 |
| cube-merged-50.8 | one face down | top-face chamber ledge | 1.4 | the top-face pocket's neck-to-chamber step overhangs 1.40 mm on each side under the neck (neck 10.0 widening to chamber 12.8) |
| cube-merged-50.8 | one face down | side pockets (4x) | 4.2 | the four vertical side faces reproduce the wall-standing/-lying bridge cases above, one per face |

What to look at after printing:

- **Sagging over the mouth**: the 14 mm mouth bridge (tile flat, wall standing) is the widest
  unsupported span in the set; check the mouth opening is still the right width and not drooping
  into the taper.
- **Elephant's foot at the lead-in**: the 0.4 mm chamfer sits right at the first layers on the
  contact face; over-squished first layers will fill it in and the clip will not lead in cleanly.
- **Post adhesion**: the post is a small-diameter (Ø2.0) column bridging from the bed/floor to the
  top plate (tile) or standing alone across a slot (wall/cube side-lock); if it does not adhere well
  on layer 1 it can lean or snap off before the top plate closes over it.
- **First-layer squish in the clip thickness**: the thinnest clip (3.5 mm, REF) is 17-18 layers at
  0.2 mm; a fat first layer eats a larger fraction of that than it does on the tile, so check the
  clip still measures its nominal thickness with calipers before fitting it.
